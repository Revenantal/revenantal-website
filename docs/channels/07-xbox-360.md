# CH 07 — XBOX 360

## Summary

The Halo 3 main menu with its music, as if the TV's input were an Xbox 360 left sitting on the menu. A tribute modelled on [halome.nu](https://halome.nu/). Approved by the owner on 2026-10-20 as the first Phase 4 build.

- **Kind:** toy · **Dial:** 7 · **Folder:** `xbox-360` · **Build:** task 4.1 · **Status:** plan approved; waiting on the owner's recording

## Concept

Tune in and the real Halo 3 main menu fills the screen, its music playing, exactly as the game shows it. Nothing to click and nothing to win: it is something to leave running on the TV, the way people left the menu running on their own TVs.

## Data Shown

None. The channel uses no site data.

## Interaction

- None inside the channel. The dial, panel buttons, settings and power keep working; no keyboard capture.
- The notice link is a real link that opens the Rules in a new tab.

## Look

- A seamless muted video loop of the menu filling 760×570 (full-bleed), from the owner's recording.
- A still frame from the same recording shows while the video loads, and in its place with reduced motion.
- A small notice at the very bottom, in Microsoft's required form with a link to the Rules: "Halo 3 © Microsoft Corporation. revenantal was created under Microsoft's 'Game Content Usage Rules' using assets from Halo 3, and it is not endorsed by or affiliated with Microsoft."
- No Xbox logos or Microsoft branding; "XBOX 360" is a referential channel label.
- Display profile: TV defaults with `bloom: false` (the notice is small text).

## Sound

The Halo 3 main menu music, 3–4 minutes, as the channel's `music` loop through the host: it fades in after the static, ducks under the OSD, and follows volume and SND. Taken from the owner's recording or from the official Halo 3 soundtrack. Microsoft's Game Content Usage Rules make Halo 3's music available for non-profit ventures.

## Build Notes

- **Asset rules:** footage and music come from recording the running game or the official soundtrack, never from game files (the Rules forbid reverse engineering). Raw recordings stay out of git (`.cache/xbox-360/`); `tools/assets/` gets a script that cuts the loop, still and music, so they can be rebuilt.
- **Media:** video about 1–2 MB (WebM, with MP4 if Safari needs it), still about 100 KB, music about 1 MB (mono AAC). Owner-approved exception to the toy art budget and the 300 KB loop budget; everything loads only on channel 7. Code stays well under 80 KB.
- **Lifecycle:** the video plays only while `active` and pauses when inactive, hidden or unmounted; no timers while inactive. The shared contract gains a narrow allowance for one muted decorative video, as the Guide has one for its weather requests.
- **Credits:** Art and Sound department entries for the recording and the music (recorded by revenantal; Halo 3 © Microsoft; used under the Game Content Usage Rules), added with the assets.

## Acceptance

- Channel 7 shows the menu loop with its music; the loop and the music repeat without a visible or audible seam.
- The notice is readable at the bottom, and its link opens the Rules in a new tab.
- The video pauses when the channel is inactive or the tab is hidden, and stops on tune-away.
- Reduced motion shows the still; screen readers hear "Xbox 360: the Halo 3 main menu with its theme" and the notice.
- The shared contract passes, the media loads only on channel 7, and the credits list the recording and music.
